\subsection{Runtime}

The harness records per-item timing; measured control runs on the T4
give 23.8 tokens/s for SmolLM2-1.7B, 10.8 for Qwen2.5-3B, and 19.9 for
Phi-3.5-mini (appendix \cref{tab:latency}). Committed vLLM cells store
0.0 timing placeholders; following the repository's cost model we treat
these as UNAVAILABLE rather than zero, so we make no backend latency
comparison. Quality--latency trade-offs are engineering-adjacent and
are kept out of the ranking analyses.
