# Editorial review — three simulated perspectives

These are internal review perspectives generated by the same assistant, not independent external peer review. They do not certify acceptance or novelty.

## A. Senior ML researcher
- Strengths: paired raw predictions, frozen task slices, an interpretable change in top-two ordering.
- Weaknesses/unsupported claims: strict control and causal attribution are overstated; model/prompt selection is narrow; no new methodological principle demonstrated.
- Method/statistics: configuration range is not a variance component; changing reasoning instructions is not meaning-preserving formatting; repeated greedy seeds do not add independent evidence.
- Missing baselines/experiments: matched-environment reruns, random prompt/exemplar selection, protocol/extractor comparison, broader models.
- Reproducibility: unpinned weights and missing historical source prevent exact inference replay.
- Likely rejection reason: small descriptive result presented as general framework validation.
- Fix: title and contributions narrowed to a preliminary artifact-based case study; causal attribution removed; confirmation listed as required.

## B. Evaluation/reproducibility researcher
- Strengths: outputs permit rescoring and pairing; failures and unavailable timing can be retained honestly.
- Weaknesses/unsupported claims: hash omits relevant context; saved settings do not prove byte-identical prompts; small malformed count does not validate extraction.
- Method/statistics: rounded score subtraction and pre-correction p rounding; Wilson overlap misinterpreted; configuration bootstrap is not item uncertainty; related GSM8K/MGSM items violate naive independence.
- Missing experiments: output adjudication, prompt/token archival, cluster-aware analysis after recovering source-question links, full environment capture.
- Reproducibility concern: token-presence validator was insufficient; main tables missing from manuscript inputs.
- Likely rejection reason: advertised reproducibility exceeds archived provenance.
- Fix: exact-count analysis, paired intervals, explicit assumptions and separate test families; automated deterministic artifact validation; no promise of exact historic inference.

## C. Skeptical novelty reviewer
- Strengths: small case study can be useful if findings and software are inspectable.
- Weaknesses: prompt-dependent scores/ranks are established by FormatSpread and multi-prompt evaluation work. ERS weights are arbitrary and pooling repeated questions makes its CI component suspect.
- Unsupported claims: first/novel metric or general leaderboard reliability; guarantees from resampling selected configs.
- Method/statistics: arbitrary configuration frequency weights, model selection sensitivity, no held-out validation. Non-significance does not prove equivalence.
- Missing baselines: existing harness protocol, multi-prompt sampling, held-out validation of any diagnostic.
- Likely rejection reason: incremental findings with an unvalidated composite.
- Fix: explicit related-work positioning, ERS omitted from central results and described only as proposed tooling; report ranking stability as empirical fractions over the selected matrix.

## Revision disposition

The active manuscript is narrowed to descriptive evidence and includes exact-count paired analyses, limitations, and missing-experiment gates. No new GPU inference was run. Automated checks do not substitute for an external methods review, bibliography completion, author approval, licensing review or visual PDF inspection. Those remain submission gates.
